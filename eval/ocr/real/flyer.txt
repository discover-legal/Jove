Flamingo Noir Record Bar Presents
SUNSET SESSIONS
ft. Al Connelly
ABOUT THE ARTIST:
There are few guitarists whose playing is more
deeply woven into the fabric of Canadian rock than
Al Connelly. A founding member of Newmarket's
own Glass Tiger, Connelly helped shape the
sound of a band that exploded onto the
international stage with its 1986 debut, The Thin
Red Line. The quadruple-platinum album produced
enduring hits including "Don't Forget Me (When I'm
Gone)" and "Someday," both of which reached the
Top 10 on the U.S. Billboard charts. Glass Tiger
went on to sell more than five million albums
worldwide, earn a Grammy nomination, win five
JUNO Awards, and share stages and studios with
artists including: Tina Turner, Rod Stewart, Bryan
Adams, Journey and Cheap Trick. The band was
inducted into Canada's Walk of Fame in 2023 and
the Canadian Music Hall of Fame in 2026.
For the Sunset Sessions, Connelly brings that
remarkable history into an intimate, stripped-back
setting, joined by Craig Riley (percussion) and
Mike Gresco (bass). Together, the trio will revisit
songs and stories from a career spanning more
than four decades, offering a rare opportunity to
hear new original music and experience the
musicianship behind some of Canada's most
recognizable rock records up close and personal.
